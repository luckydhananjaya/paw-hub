Paw Note Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026
Developer: Lucky Dhananjaya

Paw Note is a note-taking app that works without an account. This policy explains notes stored on your device, optional folder backups, and advertising services included in the Android app.

Your notes

Note titles, text, checklist items, selected animal themes, pinned status, and edit times are stored in the app on your device. Paw Note does not send note contents to a developer-operated server, AI service, or advertising provider. There is no Paw Note cloud account or server database.

Folder backups

If you choose Backup or Restore, Android lets you select a folder and grants the app access to it. Paw Note writes JSON copies of your notes to that folder, reads copies you select for restoration, and keeps up to 20 backup versions. Automatic folder backup runs while the app is open after you enable it with Backup.

A selected folder may be on your device or exposed by a storage provider. If you choose a cloud provider, that provider handles transfers, authentication, security, retention, and deletion under its own terms and privacy policy. Paw Note does not receive your cloud password. Cloud availability and sync depend on the provider.

Backup JSON files contain readable note text and are not encrypted by Paw Note. Protect access to the folder and do not share backups containing private information. Uninstalling the app does not delete external folder backups. A local folder alone does not protect against loss of the device.

Advertising and network data

The Android build includes Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK and is currently configured with Google's test ad identifiers. Test ads do not earn advertising revenue. Do not assume that test-ad mode prevents all SDK network activity.

Google's advertising SDK may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account-related identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Note contents are not supplied to this SDK by Paw Note. Google describes its handling of these data and encryption in transit in its SDK data disclosure and privacy policy.

You can manage or reset advertising identifiers using Android privacy settings. Real advertising and any required regional consent configuration must be reviewed before changing this test configuration.

Permissions and device storage

Paw Note uses Android's folder picker rather than requesting access to every file on your device. Internet and network-state permissions support the included advertising SDK and provider-dependent services. Paw Note does not include features that request camera, microphone, contacts, or precise-location access.

Retention and deletion

Delete individual notes in the app. Local automatic snapshots may retain earlier copies. To remove all app-local notes, snapshots, and preferences, clear Paw Note's app storage in Android settings or uninstall it. Separately delete PawNote JSON backups in the selected folder and any cloud trash or retained versions using your storage provider. Device-level backup and restore, if enabled by Android, follows your Android backup settings.

There is no Paw Note account to delete. The developer cannot retrieve or delete copies kept only on your device or in your chosen provider. Advertising data is controlled by Google under its policies.

Security

Access to app-local data relies on Android's app isolation and your device lock and security settings. Folder access is granted through Android. No storage method is guaranteed secure; avoid keeping sensitive secrets in an unencrypted note or backup.

Children

Paw Note is a general note-taking app and is not specifically designed for children. Store audience settings and advertising configuration must match the intended audience before distribution.

Contact and changes

For support or privacy inquiries, contact Lucky Dhananjaya through the Paw Note support tracker. A GitHub account is required to submit an issue. Issues are public: do not attach private notes, backups, passwords, or personal identifiers. You may request a private contact channel without including sensitive details.

This policy will be updated when the app's data practices change. The effective date identifies the latest revision.
